Interactive student directory powered by the StudentRecord and StudentCohort domain classes.

Why Object-Oriented Architecture Beats Raw Dictionaries (BL5 Evaluation)

In educational data analytics, working with raw dictionaries or bare rows creates silent type errors, duplicated grade-banding logic, and poor maintainability. Below is the justification for our custom StudentRecord implementation:

1. Encapsulated Business Rules
Properties like .grade_letter, .is_passing, and .improvement encapsulate Portuguese education rules (0-20 scale) in one authoritative class.
2. Special (Dunder) Methods
__len__ counts valid assessments; __getitem__ allows dict-style key access; __iter__ yields key-value tuples; __repr__ produces readable debugging output.
3. Collection Protocol (Cohort)
StudentCohort encapsulates sequences of students with len(), slicing [0:10], filtering by subject, and calculating cohort-wide pass rates.
