Outlier Detection Evaluation: Z-Score vs. Tukey's IQR Method (BL5)

A core requirement of Bloom Level 5 (Evaluation) is assessing two competing algorithmic designs against clear statistical criteria.

Method 1: Standardized Z-Score (|z| > 2.0)

Calculates distance from mean scaled by standard deviation: z = (x − μ) / σ.

  • Outliers Flagged: {{ outlier_eval.zscore_count }} records
  • Distribution Assumption: Assumes data is normally distributed (Gaussian).
  • Masking Vulnerability: The mean (μ) and standard deviation (σ) are themselves pulled by extreme outliers, which artificially inflates σ and masks borderline anomalies.
Method 2: Tukey's IQR Fences (1.5 × IQR)

Non-parametric fences: Lower = Q1 − 1.5×IQR, Upper = Q3 + 1.5×IQR.

  • Outliers Flagged: {{ outlier_eval.iqr_count }} records
  • Distribution Assumption: Non-parametric; makes zero distributional assumptions.
  • Resistant Estimators: Percentiles (Q1, Q3) are immune to extreme tail values, avoiding outlier masking.
