Key Analytical Finding (BL4/BL5): There is a statistically observable positive association between reported weekly study time and final assessment score (r = +{{ "%.3f"|format(stats.studytime_g3_correlation) }}). However, study time accounts for only ~2.6% of the variance (R2 ≈ 0.026). The positive trend survives outlier removal (strengthening to r = +0.189 when excluding 53 exam dropouts). Crucially, correlation does not establish causation; foundational prior attainment (G1/G2) and attendance play dominant roles.
