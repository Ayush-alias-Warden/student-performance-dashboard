Curricular Variance Analysis (BL4)

Mathematics presents greater academic risk: The standard deviation in Math final grades (4.58) is 42% higher than in Portuguese (3.23). Quantitative STEM topics exhibit cumulative dependency: falling behind in early algebra makes subsequent calculus concepts intractable.

In contrast, Portuguese language grades follow a tighter bell curve with fewer extreme failures, indicating that language evaluations accommodate continuous coursework and baseline literacy more forgivingly.

Cohort Trajectory (G1 to G3)

Grade persistence over the academic year: Correlation between G1 (Period 1) and G3 (Final Exam) exceeds 0.80 across both courses. This means a student's initial evaluation in the first two months of the term is the single most accurate predictor of their year-end score.

Early diagnostic screening is therefore vastly more actionable than waiting for post-exam remedial interventions.
